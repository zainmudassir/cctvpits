CCTV Installation

At CCTV PITS, we specialize in providing reliable CCTV solutions to safeguard your home or business. Whether you’re looking for a basic surveillance setup or a fully integrated security system, our expert team will ensure you have the right solution for your needs.

Our CCTV Installation Services Include:
  • Site Assessment & Consultation
  • Customized Security Solutions
  • High-Quality Cameras
  • Installation & Setup
  • Remote Viewing Access
  • Maintenance & Support

CCTV Maintenance

A CCTV system only protects you when it is working. We inspect, clean and repair existing systems so your cameras keep recording clearly and nothing fails when you need the footage.

Our CCTV Maintenance Services Include:
  • Camera Lens & Housing Cleaning
  • Cable & Connector Checks
  • Recorder & Hard Disk Health Check
  • Fault Finding & Repair
  • Replacement of Faulty Parts
  • Image Quality & Angle Adjustment

Monitoring Services

Keep an eye on your home, shop or office from wherever you are. We set up live viewing and playback on your phone and computer so you can check your property at any time.

Our Monitoring Services Include:
  • Live Viewing on Your Phone
  • Playback of Recorded Footage
  • Motion Alert Setup
  • Multiple Camera & Site Viewing
  • User Access & Password Setup
  • Guidance on Using the App

System Design

A good CCTV system starts with a good plan. We study your property and design a layout that covers the important areas without wasting cameras or cable.

Our System Design Services Include:
  • Site Survey
  • Camera Placement Planning
  • Camera Type & Number Selection
  • Cabling & Power Layout
  • Recorder & Storage Sizing
  • Clear Equipment List Before Work Starts

Storage and Backup

Footage is only useful if it is saved and can be found when you need it. We size your storage to match your cameras and help you keep copies of important recordings safe.

Our Storage and Backup Services Include:
  • Hard Disk Selection & Sizing
  • Recording Schedule Setup
  • Recording Days Planning
  • Backup to External Drive
  • Playback & Export Guidance
  • Regular Storage Health Checks

System Upgrade

You do not always need to replace everything. We upgrade existing CCTV systems with better cameras, recorders and storage, and add more cameras to what you already have.

Our System Upgrade Services Include:
  • Upgrade to HD or IP Cameras
  • Adding More Cameras
  • Recorder Replacement
  • Storage Upgrade
  • Firmware & Software Updates
  • Phone Viewing Setup

System Configuration

The right settings make a big difference to how well your cameras work. We configure your recorder, cameras and network so everything records properly and is easy to use.

Our System Configuration Services Include:
  • Recorder & Camera Setup
  • Network & Remote Access Setup
  • Resolution & Recording Settings
  • Motion Detection Zones
  • User Accounts & Passwords
  • Date, Time & Schedule Setup

System Consultation

Not sure what you need? We visit your place, listen to your needs and recommend a system that fits your space and budget, with no pressure to buy.

Our System Consultation Services Include:
  • Free Estimation
  • Site Visit & Assessment
  • Camera Type Advice (HD, IP or Wi-Fi)
  • Number of Cameras Needed
  • Brand & Package Recommendations
  • Budget Planning
